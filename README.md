# STAAD.Pro Projects

Reinforced-concrete building analysis and foundation design in **STAAD.Pro V8i**, with foundation drawings prepared in **AutoCAD**.

## G+4 RC Building – Analysis, Concrete Design & Foundation Design

A five-storey (ground + 4) reinforced-concrete frame taken from analysis through to foundation drawings.

### Workflow

1. Modelled the frame geometry, member properties and supports in STAAD.Pro.
2. Applied loads and load combinations, then ran the analysis.
3. Designed the concrete beams and columns and exported the design results.
4. Designed the foundations from the support reactions and produced the calculation report.
5. Prepared the foundation GA and detail drawings in AutoCAD.

## Opening the files

Open `G+4.std` in **STAAD.Pro V8i** or later. The PDFs and drawings can be viewed without STAAD.
