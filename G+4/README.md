# G+4 Residential RC Building: Project Files

Project folder for the five-storey RC frame analysed and designed in **STAAD.Pro V8i SELECTseries 6**, with isolated footings designed in **STAAD.Foundation 5.3** and drawings in **AutoCAD**. The full design summary (basis, loads, results and review notes) is in the [repository README](../README.md).

**Status:** ✅ Complete.

![Foundation general arrangement](Foundation%20GA.png)

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Plan / height | 15 m × 12 m, 5 × 4 bays at 3 m · G + 4, roof at +15.3 m |
| Members | Columns 600 × 400 mm · beams 400 × 400 mm · 150 mm slabs |
| Materials | M30 concrete, Fe415 reinforcement |
| Codes | IS 456:2000 · IS 1893 seismic · 21 IS load combinations |
| Base shear | 1,044 kN (T = 0.58 s, Sa/g = 2.344) |
| Quantities | 227.8 m³ concrete · ≈ 16.1 t beam and column steel |
| Foundations | 30 isolated footings, 3.05–4.10 m square, 356–507 mm thick |

## How to open

- Open `G+4.std` in STAAD.Pro V8i or CONNECT Edition and run the analysis.
- PDFs and PNGs open directly on GitHub; `.dwg` files open in AutoCAD 2018+ or the free Autodesk Viewer.
